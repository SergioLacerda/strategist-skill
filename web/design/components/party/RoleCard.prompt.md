Party-member / role card — circular sigil medallion, grimoire role name, uppercase klass label, and description. Use to present the pipeline's roles (Strategist, Ranger, Archivist, Sniper, Wizard) or any "character/agent" entity.

```jsx
<RoleCard glyph="☉" role="Strategist" klass="Master · Orchestrator" feature>
  Leads the mission end to end. Selects knowledge by task_type, enforces mission_mode, and guards the approval gate.
</RoleCard>
<RoleCard glyph="⌖" role="Ranger" klass="Scout · Discovery">Explores the terrain and returns a discovery report.</RoleCard>
```

Variants: `gate` (dashed orange approval-gate styling), `feature` (spans full grid width). Drop `glyph` for a plain card.
